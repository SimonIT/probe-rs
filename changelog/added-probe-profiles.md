Probe profiles describe how a configurable probe's adapter is wired, independently of the target. FTDI profiles follow OpenOCD's `ftdi layout_init`/`layout_signal` and enable GPIO-driven nTRST/nSRST resets. Built-in profiles are matched by USB VID/PID/product string or selected with `--probe-profile <name|path>`; the Digilent HS1/HS2/HS3 layouts are now built-in profiles, and an `axm0432` profile (e.g. Redbee Econotag) was added.
